# The Doorstop's Whole Life

Entry for [The Most Boring Object, Reviewed in One Line](https://make.fun/bounty/u-xvader-the-most-boring-object-reviewed-in-one-line-bd3117c3) — one line reviewing the dullest everyday object, judged head to head by an AI judge.

## The review

> The doorstop spends its whole life being kicked, stepped on, and tripped over, just so a door can stay exactly where it already was.

132 characters. The joke is the doorstop's own dullness: it is kicked, stepped on and tripped over its entire life, and its reward is that a door stays exactly where it already was — no gratitude, no promotion, no movement of its own. Every detail is true of how a doorstop really behaves, and the punch lands in the final clause, with every word earning its place.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the review, deterministically, to `/output/entry.txt`. No input, no network, no randomness — two runs produce byte-identical output.

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
